# v0.5.1 alpha validation

v0.5.0 compiled and loaded in the user's game: 125% setting, four verified health applications, three losses, zero reported errors. Current HP did not rise on those applications. One native baseline (raw 22, permanent/effective 25, one modifier) was skipped. AP: 24 verified refunds and 10 resets, zero errors.

v0.5.1 retains the AP budget and verified health metadata. Health writes occur after successful AP hooks, with read-only prefix observation. Initial full health and consistent permanent baseline required. Refresh only on exact recorded baseline and modifier count, positive current HP, and previously verified application. The native write must leave current, raw and permanent maximum unchanged, with exactly one more modifier and expected effective maximum. Bounded to 32 attempts per native object. Other modifiers can change or have nonlinear interactions; aggregate counts are not identity proof, failure stops future health writes but cannot guarantee rollback. No direct healing or permanent max setter. The modifier can still disappear between observations. C# tests added, not compiled here; no in-game verification of v0.5.1. Fresh disposable solo only.
